How selling works.

Six steps from price update to keys. Most sales take 2 to 4 months in total.

  1. 1

    Day 1

    Price update

    Leigh walks through your home and the sales around it, and gives you a realistic price range. Free, no obligation.

  2. 2

    Week 1

    Plan and sign

    Agree the price strategy, marketing and fees in writing. In Queensland that’s the Form 6 agency agreement.

  3. 3

    Weeks 1–2

    Get it ready

    Small fixes and styling advice, then professional photos, floor plan and video.

  4. 4

    Weeks 2–5

    Launch and open homes

    Live on realestate.com.au, Domain and NGU. Feedback after every open home, without you having to chase it.

  5. 5

    When offers arrive

    Offers and contract

    Leigh negotiates, you decide. Once a contract is signed, the buyer usually has finance and building and pest conditions to meet.

  6. 6

    Usually 30–60 days after contract

    Leigh keeps you updated until the keys are handed over.

Every sale is different. Timings are typical, not promised.
